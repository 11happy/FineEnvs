## What the community built

Within a week of part 1, two people had built on it. Edgar Arakelyan's live port is the previous chapter. Bhavish

### RailSimEnv, by Bhavish Pahwa

In [RailSimEnv](https://huggingface.co/spaces/bpHigh/RailSimEnv) the agent dispatches Arriva's trains on the
single-track Groningen–Leeuwarden line in the Netherlands after a real 2025 breakdown, collision, or signal or points
failure. On a single track, trains in opposite directions can only pass where the line doubles, so one closed section backs up
the whole line. The
agent holds, short-turns and cancels trains so they cross safely, and every plan is graded against a CP-SAT-proven
optimum. It is built from Rijden de Treinen's open train archive and OpenStreetMap, and it runs on OpenEnv with a 3D
replay.

On 45 hard eval tasks, as reported by the author:

| Model | Reward | Legal plans | At the optimum |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Qwen3.8-2.4T-A95B | 0.556 | 39 of 45 | 12 |
| DeepSeek V4.1 Flash | 0.534 | 33 of 45 | 12 |
| Claude Sonnet 5.5 | 0.467 | 45 of 45 | 4 |
| GLM-5.3-Flash | 0.360 | 24 of 45 | 6 |

An open model leads here. The one closed model keeps every plan legal but rarely finds the best one. The code is in
[bp-high/RLSimEnvs](https://github.com/bp-high/RLSimEnvs).

### Add yours

The catalog takes environments whose code lives in FineEnvs and ones that stay in their authors' own repos. Every
entry has a manifest that credits its authors, a short card, and an eval card in a shared format. That shared format
is what lets this article draw each environment's eval the same way, like the PortSimEnv v2–v5 chart above.
[Here is how to add one](https://github.com/adithya-s-k/FineEnvs/tree/main/07-simulation-environments#add-your-environment).
