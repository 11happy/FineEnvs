## Ongoing work

<HtmlEmbed src="d3-simrl-roadmap.html" frameless />

Most of the first roadmap is built now, and most of it by the community. A live port with news over time, pilots and
tugs, weather history and ship-to-port talk all exist as PortSimEnv v2–v5, and RailSimEnv brought a second job. What
is still open: post-training on these environments, more data (AIS tracks, terminal logs, other ports) and more
jobs worth simulating from public records.

The question behind the series is still the same. We can build realistic environments from seed data, but how do we
get them as close to the real world as possible? If you have an idea or an environment, add it to the
[catalog](https://github.com/adithya-s-k/FineEnvs/tree/main/07-simulation-environments) or start a thread in the
[GitHub discussion](https://github.com/adithya-s-k/FineEnvs/discussions/36).
